Key Technique 01

NeRF · Neural Radiance Fields

How NeRF renders one pixel
Core Idea
Encode a 3D scene inside an MLP. Given position (x,y,z) + direction (θ,φ), predict color + density. Integrate along rays → pixel color.
NeRF MLP Fθ(x, y, z, θ, φ) → (σ, RGB)
How It Works
1
Cast Rays
One ray per pixel from the camera through the scene.
2
Sample & Query MLP
Pick 3D points along each ray; ask the MLP for σ and RGB.
3
Volume Rendering
Integrate color & density along the ray → pixel color.
4
Train on 2D Photos
Minimize pixel error vs. real photos from known camera poses.
