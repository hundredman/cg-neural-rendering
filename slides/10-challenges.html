Challenges & Open Problems

01
Generalization Across Scenes
Most models train per-scene from scratch. A single model that generalizes to new scenes without retraining remains unsolved.
Active research
02
Dynamic & Moving Scenes
NeRF assumes a static world. Handling fast motion — sports, crowds — faithfully remains very challenging.
Major open challenge
03
Scene Editing
Neural scenes are hard to edit — moving an object or changing a texture is not straightforward. Making them artist-friendly is key.
Open problem
04
Memory & Mobile Compute
3DGS requires hundreds of MB–GB per scene. Running on phones or embedded devices is still an engineering challenge.
Improving rapidly
