Wrap-Up

Summary & Future Outlook

Key Takeaways
1
Classical rendering requires explicit 3D geometry — powerful but brittle for real-world capture.
2
Implicit Neural Representations encode scenes as learned functions — continuous, no explicit mesh.
3
NeRF (2020) trains an MLP on 2D photos → photo-realistic novel views. But slow to train and render.
4
3DGS (2023) uses explicit Gaussians → real-time rendering without sacrificing quality.
5
The field evolves fast: Instant NGP, generative NeRF, 3D foundation models reshape what's possible each year.
Future Directions
3D Foundation Models
A single pretrained model that understands any 3D scene — no per-scene training.
Real-Time & Mobile
Compression + hardware → neural rendering on phones and AR glasses.
Text-to-3D Content
"Describe a scene in words → get a fully rendered 3D world" is already emerging.
Physics Integration
Neural rendering + simulators → environments that look and behave realistically.
Neural rendering is not just a new algorithm — it is a new way of representing and understanding the 3D world from visual data. The gap between captured reality and computer graphics is closing fast.
