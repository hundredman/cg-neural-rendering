Classical Method

Photogrammetry — the old way

1
Take 100–1000 overlapping photos
2
Find matching points across photos
3
Triangulate → build a 3D mesh
4
Project photos as texture onto mesh
✅ Works well for
Buildings Statues Archaeology Game assets
❌ Breaks on
Hair & fur Glass & mirrors Smoke Shiny metal Plants & leaves
The core problem
A mesh is rigid. It can't represent anything soft, fuzzy, or view-dependent.
