Evaluation

Pros & Cons

✅ What it does well
🌿
Complex materials
Hair, smoke, glass, fur — things that break photogrammetry work naturally.
📱
Just a phone camera
No LiDAR, no depth sensor. A smartphone is enough to capture a scene.
💡
Correct lighting from every angle
Reflections and highlights look right — the network learns how light behaves.
⚡
Real-time rendering (3DGS)
30+ fps — fast enough for VR headsets and live applications.
❌ Current limitations
🔒
One model = one scene
Training a NeRF gives you only that one scene. New scene → train again from scratch.
✏️
Hard to edit
Can't easily move objects, change textures, or animate — the scene is "baked in."
🏃
Moving objects cause artifacts
Running people, flowing water → blurring and ghosting. Still very hard to solve.
💾
Large file sizes (3DGS)
Hundreds of MB per scene — too large for most phones today.
Great at capturing appearance from photos — still catching up on editing, animation, and scale.
