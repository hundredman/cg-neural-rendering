Motivation

Why Neural Rendering?

Limitations of Classical Methods
Hard to capture real-world scenes
Creating 3D geometry manually is expensive, slow, and error-prone.
Meshes can't represent fuzzy materials
Hair, smoke, glass, clouds don't fit triangle meshes well.
Novel view synthesis is unsolved
Rendering from a new camera angle, given only photos, remains hard.
What Neural Rendering Offers
Learn directly from photos
No explicit 3D model needed — just images from multiple angles.
Implicit, flexible representation
A neural network encodes geometry + appearance + lighting in one function.
Render from any viewpoint
Once trained, generate views the network never directly saw.
Key Insight
"What if we let a neural network learn the scene from photographs — no manual 3D modeling needed?"
