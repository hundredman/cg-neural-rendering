The Big Idea

What is Neural Rendering?

Instead of building a 3D mesh,
train a neural network to memorize the scene —
then ask it to render from any angle.
🧠
The network IS the 3D model
No mesh. No point cloud. The scene lives inside the network's weights.
📸
Input: just photos
Give it ~20–100 photos from known angles. It learns from them automatically.
🎥
Output: any viewpoint
Ask the trained network to render the scene from a new angle — it generates the image.
