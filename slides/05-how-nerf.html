How It Works · Part 1

NeRF · Neural Radiance Fields (2020)

How NeRF renders one pixel
1
Input: ~20–100 photos + camera positions
You take photos of the scene from many angles. A separate algorithm figures out where each camera was.
2
Train the network to reproduce the photos
The network tries to render each training photo. It adjusts its weights until the rendered image matches the real one.
3
Render from any new camera angle
Once trained, tell the network a new camera position → it renders a photo-realistic image from that viewpoint.
4
Key insight: view-dependent appearance
The network takes viewing direction as input, so reflections and highlights are handled naturally — unlike a mesh.
What the network does position + direction → density + color
