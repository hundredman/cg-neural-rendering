NeRF · Results & Limitations

What NeRF Gets Right
Photo-realistic novel views
New viewpoints look convincingly real — including complex view-dependent effects like specular highlights.
Fine detail from sparse inputs
Trained on ~20–100 photos, reconstructs intricate geometry: plant leaves, fur, translucent objects.
No explicit 3D model needed
Only camera poses + images. No depth sensors, no manual modeling.
Limitations
Extremely slow training
Original NeRF: 1–2 days to train a single scene on a high-end GPU.
Slow rendering per image
~30 seconds per image — thousands of MLP evaluations per ray. Not real-time.
Per-scene overfitting
Each model = one scene. New scene = train from scratch. Does not generalize.
