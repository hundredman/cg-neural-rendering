The Problem

Capturing Reality is Hard

The goal
Take photos of a real place or object → render it from any angle, like a video game.
📸
Example: you take 100 photos of a building
Can a computer figure out what the building looks like from an angle you never photographed?
🎬
Example: film VFX
Scan an actor's face → render it from any camera angle in post-production, without reshoots.
Why is this hard?
A photo loses depth
A camera collapses 3D into 2D — depth information is gone. One photo can't tell you the shape of an object.
Light is complicated
The same surface looks different from different angles — reflections, shadows, and transparency all change.
Missing information
Parts of the scene hidden from the camera can't be seen in any photo — yet we want to render them too.
The big question
Can a neural network fill in the gaps and reconstruct a full 3D scene from 2D photos alone?
