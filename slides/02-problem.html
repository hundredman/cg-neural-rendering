The Problem

Why is capturing reality hard?

You took 100 photos of a building.
Can the computer show it from an angle you never photographed?
📷
Photos lose depth
A camera collapses 3D into 2D. One photo can't tell you how far away anything is.
💡
Light is complicated
The same surface looks different from every angle — reflections, shadows, transparency all change.
🙈
Some parts are hidden
Anything behind or below the object is invisible in photos — yet we want to render it anyway.
