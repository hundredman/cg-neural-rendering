Where We Are

Limits & What's Coming

Still unsolved
No universal model
Each NeRF knows only one scene. We don't yet have a "GPT for 3D."
Moving objects fail
Anything moving during capture — people, water — causes ghosting.
Can't edit or animate
You can't drag objects around like in a game engine. The scene is frozen.
City-scale is still hard
Scaling to large outdoor environments without artifacts is an open problem.
Where it's heading
🌐
3D Foundation Models
One model understands any 3D scene from a few photos — already emerging.
📱
On-device rendering
Compression research is making 3DGS small enough to run on phones.
✍️
Text → 3D scene
"Describe a scene" → AI generates a fully renderable 3D world.
🤖
Robotics
Neural scenes give robots a rich map of the real world for navigation.
The gap between a photo and a 3D world is closing fast.
