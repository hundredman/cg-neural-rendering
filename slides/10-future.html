Where We Are

Current Limits & What's Next

Still unsolved
No generalization
Each NeRF model only knows one scene. We don't yet have a "universal 3D model" like GPT is for language.
Can't handle moving objects
Anything that moves during capture — people walking, water flowing — causes ghosting artifacts.
Hard to edit or animate
The scene is stored in a neural network. You can't just drag objects around like you can in a game engine.
Scale: city-level is still hard
Scaling to entire city blocks or outdoor scenes without artifacts is an active research problem.
Where it's heading
🌐
3D Foundation Models
Like GPT for text, a single model that understands any 3D scene from a few photos — already emerging.
📱
On-device & real-time
Compression research is making 3DGS small enough to run on phones — photorealistic AR from your camera.
✍️
Text-to-3D scene
"Describe a scene" → AI generates a fully renderable 3D world. DreamFusion already does early versions of this.
🤖
Robotics & simulation
Neural scenes give robots a rich, queryable map of the real world — enabling smarter navigation and manipulation.
The gap between a photo and a 3D world is closing fast — and neural rendering is doing the closing.
